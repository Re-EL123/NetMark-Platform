Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up?

Go to the Register page, fill in your details, select your role, and submit. You'll receive a verification email.

How do referrals work?

Share your referral link/code. When someone signs up with it, they become part of your network and you earn commissions as per the plan.

When do commissions become available?

Commissions may be held for a review period before becoming available in your wallet.

How do I withdraw?

Go to Wallet in your panel and request a withdrawal if payouts are enabled on your plan.

Need more help?

Contact us via Support or send feedback via Feedback.
